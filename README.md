Aetherfront Terra Wars full instructions:

## CONTROLS:
WASD to move
Arrow keys are used in battles for moving units and selecting attack targets
C opens up your collection panel, where you can drag cards around to change out your deck
B opens up your deck and stats panel, so you can check card formatting and your own stats
the Info button reminds you of some of this
press Space to close all active panels
press X to close a dialogue (narrative) box


## GAMEPLAY:
During your main phase, you can place one Terrain on the battlefield per turn, (drag it from your hand to the field). You can only add terrain next to existing ones or on your home row (y=1). Place terrains intentionally, because Units have specific requirements when it comes to terrain they can live on and cross when it comes to moving.

Once your turn starts and you have terrain active, you gain Aether. You gain 1 Aether for each terrain you control when the turn starts. You can use this Aether to pay for summoning costs of your Units/Tactics (or, more rarely, pay a cost for adding terrains). 

Use your main phase to place units on your terrains as applicable and place terrains. You can also play a Tactic during your turn, which is just a card with a one-use effect.

units have PassConditions, as specified on their card. Most units can only exist on land/ground, in which case they have an empty passcondition. Some units can Swim, so they can also cross water as well as land. Some units have SwimOnly, so they can only exist on liquid water (no frozen water doesn't count). Some units have flying, which means they can cross any terrain. flying units also cannot be attacked by non-flying units.

### Phases and attacking:
You have two phases: main and attacks.
During your main, click on a unit to select it. Then you can press an arrow to attempt to move your unit.
During attacks phase, when you select a unit and press an arrow key, the unit attempts to attack in that direction.

During your attack step, if you control a unit with a straight, vertical path to the other side of the board that it can cross, then you can direct an attack towards the opponent to deal them damage. When the opponent's life points hits zero, they lose.
